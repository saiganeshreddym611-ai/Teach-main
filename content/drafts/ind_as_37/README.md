# Ind AS 37 drafts

AI-generated walkthroughs of four Ind AS 37 decision trees, copied from the owner's `Teach/FR`
folder on 2026-09-27 (TASKS.md Q13). They were the starting point for
`content/packs/ind_as_37/concept_map.json` (T25).

They are drafts, not sources: nothing here is cited to students or used as evidence in a review.
Every node drafted from them is checked against the MCA text of Ind AS 37 by the T23 review.
Known gaps found while drafting: Tree_2 calls the discount rate "risk-free" (Ind AS 37 para 47
requires a pre-tax rate reflecting the risks specific to the liability), and Tree_3 omits the
cost-of-fulfilling definition added as para 68A in 2022.
