## Data shapes

```text
Claim   {claim_id, item_id, kind: rubric|explanation|example|misconception, text,
         expect: supported|contradicted}          # misconceptions expect "contradicted"
Packet  {run_id, item_id, claims[], passages[{passage_id, label, text}], coverage_question}
Answer  {claim_id, verdict: supported|contradicted|not_found, passage_id, quote}
Answers {item_id, answers[], coverage_gaps[str]}
```

Session mode writes `data/reviews/pending/<run_id>/<item_id>.md` (readable packet) and
`<item_id>.answers.json` (answer template). `--ingest <run_id>` validates and applies the answers.

## Decision rule

- A claim passes when its verdict equals `expect` and its quote is found word for word (after
  whitespace normalisation) in the cited passage. A failed quote check turns the verdict into
  `not_found`.
- `claude_reviewed` only if every claim passes and there are no coverage gaps. Otherwise
  `needs_expert`, with one reason per failed claim or gap in the report.
- Every run writes `data/reviews/<run_id>.md` and updates `reviews.json` only through
  `record_claude_review`.
