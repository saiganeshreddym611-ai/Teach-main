**Coder: Claude** (D8: domain content and a security-sensitive evaluator).
**Approves: owner** (D5: task templates are domain content). Requirements: FR-12; decisions in
`HUMAN-DECISIONS.md` section 7 (task set 7.1, grading defaults 7.3). Schemas already exist in
`tutor/contracts/skills.py` (T02).

## Plan

1. **Pack files** under `content/packs/ind_as_115/`:
   - `skills/revenue_recognition_services.json`: one `SkillDef`, "Revenue recognition for a
     service-based company", four task IDs, `unlock_rule: "all_tasks_passed"`.
   - `tasks/<task_id>.json`: four `TaskTemplate` drafts, one fictional mid-size Indian IT services
     company throughout: `rr_fixed_price_cost_to_cost`, `rr_contract_modification`,
     `rr_variable_consideration`, `rr_discrepancy_hunt` (four planted issues plus one or two clean
     rows that look suspicious).
   - Grading defaults from 7.3 in every brief: about half numbers and half judgement, the key
     treatment decision as a required item, pass mark 0.75, rupees rounded to the nearest rupee,
     tolerance 0.5% or ₹1,000 (whichever is larger), percentages to one decimal, FY April to March.
   - All values that must stay exact are decimal or ISO-date strings. No review status inside the
     files; reviews go through `reviews.json` (T11).
2. **Loader:** `tutor/skills/loader.py` with `load_skills(pack)` and `load_tasks(pack)`, and T11's
   `pack_items()` extended so skills and tasks get content hashes and review status.
3. **Safe expressions:** `tutor/skills/expr.py`, `evaluate(expr: str, params: dict[str, str]) ->
   Decimal | bool`. Parses with `ast` and allows only numbers, parameter names, `+ - * /`, unary
   minus, comparisons, `and`/`or`/`not` and parentheses. Anything else raises `ExprError`. Never
   `eval`, `exec` or attribute access.
4. **Calculator names:** `tutor/skills/registry.py` holds `CALCULATOR_NAMES`, the four names the
   templates use. T51 fills in the functions.
5. **Validator:** extend `scripts/validate_pack.py`: node IDs exist; passage IDs resolve (skipped
   with a warning while the pack has no corpus); calculator is registered; rubric weights sum to
   1.0; planted discrepancies point at real document rows; every narrative placeholder has a
   parameter; every constraint parses with `expr.py`.
