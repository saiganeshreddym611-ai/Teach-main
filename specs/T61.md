## Behaviour

1. **Chapters.** When the level-1 nodes have more than one distinct `chapter`, group the level-1
   rows under a small chapter heading, in first-seen order; `null` goes to "Other". With one
   chapter the grid looks as it does today.
2. **Omissions.** `page.tsx` stores the node IDs from the diagnostic `grade` event's `omissions`.
   The grid marks those level-1 rows with a dashed rose border and a small "not mentioned" tag
   until a later ledger event gives that subtree evidence. They are cleared on a new session.
3. **Evidence panel.** Every level-1 title and every chip is a `<button>`. Clicking one opens
   `EvidencePanel` inside the rail, in place of the node list (the header and legend stay). It
   shows:
   - title, status in plain words (`VERIFIED` "mastered", `PARTIAL` "partly shown", `GAP` "gap
     found: the student said something wrong or missed it", `NOT_MENTIONED` "not assessed yet"),
     and confidence as a percentage;
   - the student's quote: the `evidence` of the latest event for this node in `ledger` (this
     session's events);
   - if the status is not `NOT_MENTIONED` but this session has no event: "Assessed in an earlier
     session.";
   - each of that event's `passage_ids`: fetch `getPassage(id)` once per ID and cache it in
     component state; show `citation.label` in bold and the passage text below it (collapsed after
     about 6 lines with a "Show more" button). On a fetch error show "Source text unavailable"
     with the ID.
4. **Keyboard.** On open, focus moves to the panel heading; `Escape` or the "Back" button closes
   it and returns focus to the chip that opened it. The panel is a `section` with `aria-label`
   "Evidence for <title>". Every control is reachable with Tab.
5. **Live.** The panel reads from props, so a new grade updates it while it is open.
6. **Kiosk rule.** No links out, no new routes and no navigation. The passage text stays inside the
   rail.
